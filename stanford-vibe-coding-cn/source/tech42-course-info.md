## Course Description
Software development is evolving from traditional coding into intuitive collaboration between humans and AI. This course teaches you "vibe coding," where natural conversation becomes your primary programming language for creating applications ranging from budgeting systems to interactive games.

It'll equip you with critical-thinking skills for guiding AI toward precise solutions while recognizing when algorithms go astray. Through weekly case studies, you'll tackle individual challenges using platforms like Cursor, v0, or Replit, then collaborate to analyze different approaches and outcomes.

The course emphasizes strategic prompting techniques, systematic problem decomposition, and iterative refinement methods that transcend specific tools. You'll develop meta-skills, including precision in shaping AI output, judgment in assessing solutions, and building effective workflows.

By the end, you'll complete a capstone application demonstrating your systematic framework for AI-assisted development.
